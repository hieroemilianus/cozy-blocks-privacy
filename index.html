Privacy Policy — Cozy Blocks

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Cozy Blocks: ASMR Clicker ("the app") is made by Nathan Corporation ("we"). This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Information we do not collect

You can play without an account. We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, location or any other personal details. Your game progress (levels, upgrades, settings) is saved on your device. If you use the in-game "Backup code" feature, the code stays with you unless you choose to share it.

Background music notification

If you choose "Keep playing" in Settings → When I leave the app, the app keeps its music playing in the background and shows a notification with a Stop button. On Android 13 and later, the app asks for permission to show this notification. No information is sent anywhere for this feature.

Cloud save (optional, where available)

Cloud save is not available in every version of the app. Where it is available and you choose "Continue with Google" or "Continue with Apple", we use Google Firebase Authentication to create an account linked to that sign-in, and Google Cloud Firestore to store a copy of your game progress. We store your account ID, the display name or email provided by Google or Apple (only to show who is signed in), and your save data. We do not use this for advertising. You can sign out at any time, and you can delete your cloud save and account in Settings → Save & sync → Delete cloud save & account.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows optional rewarded video ads, which you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game bonus. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, general device information and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this data is described in its policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

On iPhone and iPad, Apple asks for your permission before your device's advertising identifier can be used for tracking.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

In-app purchases

You can optionally buy packs of in-game coins. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store; we never see or store your card or payment details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase succeeded so we can add the coins to your game.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available at this page.
